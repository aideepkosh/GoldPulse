# Privacy Policy for GoldPulse: Daily Gold Rate

**Effective date:** 27 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app **GoldPulse: Daily Gold Rate** ("GoldPulse", "the app", package `com.deepkosh.goldpulse`), developed by **DeepKosh** ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: **GoldPulse does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.**

## Information we collect

None. GoldPulse:

- has no user accounts or login,
- shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs,
- contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs,
- does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other personal information,
- does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other identifier.

## How the app gets gold rates

To show the gold rate, the app downloads public market prices over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection from these free services:

| Service | What the app downloads |
|---|---|
| gold-api.com | International gold spot price and the USD to INR exchange rate |
| api.frankfurter.dev | USD to INR exchange rate (European Central Bank reference rate) |
| cdn.jsdelivr.net and currency-api.pages.dev (fawazahmed0/currency-api) | Daily gold price and USD to INR exchange rate, including the previous day's values |

These requests contain no personal information. Like any connection to a website, the services can see your device's IP address and basic technical details of the request (for example, that it came from the GoldPulse app). GoldPulse has no control over these services; their own privacy policies apply.

## Information stored on your device

GoldPulse saves the following **only on your device**, so it can work offline and show the change since yesterday:

- the most recent gold price and exchange rate, with the time they were fetched,
- the gold price and exchange rate for up to the last 14 days,
- your notification setting and the date of the last daily notification.

This information never leaves your device, is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

## Notifications

On Android 13 and later, GoldPulse asks for permission to show notifications. It uses this only to show the daily gold rate notification around 9 AM. You can turn the notification off at any time in the app or in your device's system settings.

## Children's privacy

GoldPulse does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data deletion

Because GoldPulse collects no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove the data the app stores on your device, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
