## Full description (max 4000 characters)

GoldPulse shows today's indicative gold rate in India, in rupees, the moment you open it.

• 24K, 22K and 18K rates per 1 gram and per 10 grams
• Change vs yesterday, in rupees and percent
• Daily notification around 9 AM with today's 22K and 24K rate
• Works offline: the last rate is saved, with the time it was updated
• Pull down or tap refresh for the latest rate
• Light and dark theme

How the rate is calculated
GoldPulse uses the international gold spot price (XAU/USD), the USD to INR exchange rate and India's import duty. 22K is 91.6% and 18K is 75% of the 24K rate. The app shows every input, so you can see exactly how the number is worked out.

Private by design
No ads. No analytics or tracking. No login. GoldPulse does not collect any personal data.

Important
Rates are indicative only. They exclude 3% GST, making charges and local premiums, so prices at jewellers will be higher and vary by city. Always confirm the final price with your jeweller. GoldPulse is not financial advice and is not affiliated with any jeweller, bank or exchange.

Data: gold-api.com, Frankfurter (European Central Bank rates) and fawazahmed0/currency-api.
